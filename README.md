# 🚗⚡ EV Market Intelligence Dashboard

A Python-based Electric Vehicle Market Analytics Dashboard designed
to analyze EV sales, pricing, battery capacity, driving range,
vehicle types and market trends.

## 📊 Key Features

- EV sales analysis
- Brand performance analysis
- Top EV models
- Country-wise EV sales
- Year-wise sales growth
- Brand market share
- Price vs driving range
- Battery capacity vs range
- Best-value EV analysis
- Affordable vs premium EV analysis
- Vehicle type analysis
- Automated business insights

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs

- Total EV Sales
- Total EV Models
- Total Brands
- Average EV Price
- Average Driving Range
- Average Battery Capacity

## 📊 Visualizations

1. EV Sales Growth
2. Top EV Brands
3. EV Sales by Country
4. Price vs Driving Range
5. Sales by Vehicle Type
6. Sales by Price Segment

## 🛠️ Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
